---
title: Support for Istio 1.29 ends on the 12th of October, 2026
subtitle: Support Announcement
description: Upcoming Istio 1.29 end of life announcement.
publishdate: 2026-09-09
---

According to Istio's [support policy](/docs/releases/supported-releases#support-policy), minor releases like 1.29 are supported until six weeks after the N+2 minor release (1.31 in this case). [Istio 1.31 was released on the 31st of August, 2026](/news/releases/1.31.x/announcing-1.31/), and support for 1.29 will end on the 12th of October, 2026.

At that point we will stop back-porting fixes for security issues and critical bugs to 1.29, so we encourage you to upgrade to the latest version of Istio ({{<istio_release_name>}}). If you don't do this you may put yourself in the position of having to do a major upgrade on a short timeframe to pick up a critical fix.

We care about you and your clusters, so please be kind to yourself and upgrade.
