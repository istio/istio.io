The `IstioOperator` configuration, often called `IOP` or `IO`, is used when
installing Istio with `istioctl install`. It was also used by the former
[in-cluster Istio Operator](/blog/2024/in-cluster-operator-deprecation-announcement/),
which was removed in Istio 1.24. Its removal does not affect `istioctl install`.
